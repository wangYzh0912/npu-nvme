# Measurement contract

- Scope: Qwen3-8B TP4/DP1/PP1, sequence parallel, full recomputation, fixed repeated text, FP32 parameters and optimizer state, BF16 model compute. Sequence128/512/2048 and micro-batch1/2 are the registered O1 workload matrix; three independent model processes per cell, four physical cards reported separately.
- PMU activity: replay0 task cycles divided by native engine frequency, core count and window width. AIC and AIV each have their own capacity denominator. Native10ms sampling and >=10ms display bins; per-core first intervals discarded; missing coverage stays invalid. Joint high/low bins do not prove instantaneous overlap, issued FLOPs, instruction mix or reclaimable compute.
- Low-AIC time: valid-time fraction AIC<=10%. High-AIV time: AIV>=50%. Joint time and conditional low-AIC|high-AIV reported separately. Sensitivity grids use low5/10/20 and high30/50/70. A cell is called majority only if every one of three valid independent processes exceeds50%; a matrix-wide claim requires every registered cell. This is configuration prevalence within one model family, not cross-model/population prevalence.
- Quality: before collector attach, >=16 training steps and six-step CV<=5%; finite completed training. Collector35s; >=30s valid full display bins; >=95% display-bin coverage; absolute timing perturbation<=10%. HBM has a separate gate: only steps with >=95% native HBM coverage enter per-step flow means, with their counts and minimum(all-step)coverage retained; >=30s accepted complete steps and >=95% selected steps covered are required for HBM measurement acceptance. PMU and HBM quality are distinct. Invalid or failed repetitions remain recorded and do not qualify the cell.
- Time reference: AIC/AIV native parser timestamps -> job wall clock; CANN8.3 HBM additionally needs device-to-host delta from host_start/dev_start/info clock anchors. HBM counts are per interval,32bytes/count, not cumulative deltas. Native20ms means weighted by boundary overlap. Read/write per full step separate from static logical tensor bytes. No saturation inference without bandwidth calibration.
- Capacity busy milliseconds per step integrates only valid full PMU display bins, divided by selected complete step count; pmu_valid_time_fraction_of_step_windows exposes truncation of each step to complete10ms bins. It is aggregate engine capacity time, not one core duration or FLOPs.
- Timings: unprofiled pre-attach six-step median is the control; PMU sample has independent overhead test. Per-task profiling is a separate diagnostic execution. Task duration sums are never normal critical-path time.
- Numerical checks: identical initial-state hashes; within-mode repeated loss trajectories and final states. Cross-JIT common-prefix loss differences are descriptive only; no post-hoc tolerance grants full-model equivalence. O2 uses1024steps to provision sample duration, O1 uses96, both constant1e-6/no warmup; finalstates across different horizons are not compared.
- Optimizer fragments use actual installed implementations, recorded shard geometries and fixed finite synthetic values. Eight updates check param/m/v againstFloat64oracle with predeclared tolerances; ten blocks of40updates include one device synchronization per block. Resident Parameter and ordinary Tensor calls differ in graph boundary too, so their timing difference is not a pure DMA-only causal estimate. NativeLevel0 traces disclose all observed task types; no transfer-byte claim when payload is unavailable.
- Legal co-run requires runtime input/collective/alias/lifetime readiness. Static dependency graphs and idle AIC bins alone do not satisfy that gate. Global norm/clipping and activation recomputation are explicit constraints.

Experiment order is serial and fixed by stage, not randomized. Do not attribute differences solely to scheduler changes without the retained configuration/source/numerical/quality checks; the single matched O0-HBM supplement is a control, not three new independent O0 repetitions.

The supplementary real-model O1 task diagnostic is a separate40-step run, withLevel0 CPU+NPU profiling afterstep18 through22. Analysis uses complete steps19-21 on allfourcards; step22 can include profiler teardown and is excluded. Full40-step loss trajectories match the formal O1 run. Scoped kernels are joined to native task_time by device/rank,stream,task ID and exactstarttimestamp; the tail begins at namedTensorMove-op0 and ends at its lastobservedtask. Native overlap checks include tasks that started before thetail. Whole-step overhead44.6–45.9% makes its durations diagnostic only.
